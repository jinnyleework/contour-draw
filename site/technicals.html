technicals

the contour engine: how an object becomes slices, how the slices are painted, and how the cursor drives them. Every value here is the engine's own

    01pipelineengine/README.mdshape, slice, sheets, painter, svg: plain JavaScript modules, no dependencies

    1shapeA signed distance function sdf(x, y, z, prm): negative inside, positive outside, built from src/math.js (rcyl, roundBox, smin, subtract).
    2sliceslicePart samples the sdf on a grid at each height and traces the zero line with marching squares (src/contour.js).
    3sheetsA sheet is the result at one height: a list of closed loops, thinned to within a third of a pixel.
    4paintSheets drawn bottom up, each with an opaque fill so a higher one covers a lower one; then every edge weighted by rule A.
    5playOne animation loop per figure, asleep whenever nothing needs it: pointer away and settled, off screen, tab hidden, or reduced motion.

    02rule Asrc/painter.js · RULEweight and depth: one ink, its weight and opacity doing all the work

    0.85baseThe weight every edge starts from (the library's tiles draw at 0.6 of it).
    1.0contrastSilhouettes heavy, edges facing you light: an edge seen side-on gets up to 2.3 times more.
    0.8depthFarther edges are thinner and fainter.
    0.25fadeLower sheets are a little fainter than higher ones.

    Weights run from 0.2 to 3.4 screen px and never scale with the drawing; they and the opacities are bucketed (8 weights, 4 opacities), so a sheet is a handful of paths, not one per edge.

    03view · slicessrc/camera.js · src/slice.jsone orthographic camera, and slices the same distance apart in every part

    35°azimuthHow far the object is turned. The panel's turn spins it all the way round.
    0.5tiltThe sine of the elevation: 0.5 is the 2:1 view.
    600pageEvery figure is drawn on a 600 × 600 page; S scales it and zm is the height that sits mid-page.
    0.03GAPThe distance between slices. Thin upright things (stems, poles) take 0.01, or they read as dots.
    0.0144CELLThe grid the sdf is sampled on. A part with thin features sets a finer cell of its own, about a third of the thinnest one.
    staticpartsSliced once. A dynamic part is sliced again whenever its key(prm) changes, so it keeps a small box and a coarse key.

    04hoversrc/pointer.js · src/split.jsnothing moves until the pointer arrives; then a sprung cursor drives the figure

    aanimateThe cursor scrubs the figure's action: where it is over the drawing sets how far through the action it is (scene.drive(x, y), 0 to 1), and leaving lets it spring back to rest.
    bsplitThe layers part at the cursor's height: above lift, below drop, across the full width (reach 60 px). The figure itself stays at rest.
    cfollowA figure may read the cursor's place too: the terrain raises a peak under it, with a wandering peak when nothing points at it.
    dtouchA finger acts while it is down, and the pose is held 1.4 s after it lifts, so a tap reads as a look.
    edemoPlays the whole loop once. The library's auto-play plays it over and over; nothing runs on its own otherwise.

    06shapesruleswhat a figure keeps so its slices close and its parts read in the right order

    closed boxesThe sdf is positive everywhere on the edge of its box, or the contour will not close.
    one gapSlices the same GAP apart across parts, so they interleave cleanly; ties at one height go to the higher order.
    height, not frontThe painter orders by height alone. Parts that must read as nearer do not overlap on the page at the default view.
    overlayOnly for what shows through everything (a filament, a halo): it is painted last, whatever its height.
    unique namesTop-level names are unique across modules: the build joins them into one script and checks.
    budgetA moving frame under 25 ms, and its markup under 450 kB.
