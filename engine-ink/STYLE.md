# Ink style ledger

## Brief
Scratchy pen-and-ink prints, flat (2D, `VIEW.flat`, layer order covers). Bold black ink with uneven, torn and pooled edges; pen
lines that swell and thin; hatching for tone, uneven and broken; highlights carved out of solids in paper; ink spatter, dry-brush
voids and paper grain. One ink (the person: "prefer to do it just one colour"). Paper a warm off-white. Stills only (the person asked for no animation).
The engine's main job: drawings given as images are traced and redrawn in this style.

References (the person's, shared in chat): the three-panel boxers (black silhouette prints with white highlights cut in), the
red São Paulo footballers (outlined and solid figures, spatter, red on cream), the Last Supper playing card (scratchy pen lines,
hatching, red suit marks).

Primitive: an **ink field**: a shape's sdf, made rough, pooled, spread, spattered and dried, traced where it crosses zero into
filled loops. Marks: fill, line (the outline as a band), hatch (strokes clipped to the shape). Inks: black and paper.
